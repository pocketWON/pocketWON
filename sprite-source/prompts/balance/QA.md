# Balance frame review

Accepted ten unique, individually AI-generated poses: neutral, glance, half, blink, prepare, anticipate, peak, follow, settle, return. Every accepted source is a 512×512 RGBA canvas with transparent corners. Identity uses the canonical white wallet/bean body, blue-gray outline, viewer-right clasp and consistent large oval eyes. The original canonical reference was positioned once before sequencing; subsequent source imports resize the full canvas only.

Idle changes gaze and eyelids, retains the wallet/coin prop, and reverses through glance to neutral. Action anticipates by looking down and adjusting the hands, opens the wallet and exposes a coin, then returns through settle/return. Contact sheets were inspected at small display size, and the actual 6FPS idle and 10FPS action were inspected in Home.

Grounded feet remain approximately y420–421. Neutral reuse gives an exact first/last pose. Alpha and every packed cell round-trip were verified by the packer; browser timing checks sample both sides of every interval. Runtime sizes and checks are recorded in evidence/SPRITE-MOTION/assets.json and checkpoint01/verification.json.
